# Project context

Read [the canonical project brief](docs/CANONICAL_PROJECT_BRIEF.md) and
[the canonical product specifications](docs/CANONICAL_PRODUCT_SPECS.md) before
planning, designing, implementing, or writing customer-facing copy.

These documents record the founder's October 11, 2026 decisions and supersede
conflicting statements in older proposals and factory documents. Preserve the
distinction between founder decisions, verified integration facts, and unresolved
supplier facts. Do not turn a hypothesis into a measured result or a product claim.

The audience is personal senders. The priority is a thoughtful gesture made
seamless. Aster & Blanche is the recipient-facing stationery identity; its removal
from the SendMyNotes customer experience is the intended redesign direction.

There have been zero independent customer conversions as of the canonical
baseline. The one paid order was the founder's test. Apple Pay is untested.

Marketing photos may be used only after personal information is excluded or
redacted and the exported asset is visually checked. Preserve originals privately;
do not copy unsanitized assets into public output. Do not run fulfillment scripts
as an audit check: they can create billable orders and physical mail.
