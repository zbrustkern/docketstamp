# Official consumer templates and a California product area

Research: October 20–21, 2026. Product recommendation for SPRINT-0012, not provider authorization or a completed state-law audit. Primary sources were reviewed directly or through indexed official text where the agency returned 403. No third-party template was imported into the product.

## Decision

Yes: official samples support a credible service that makes a consumer-selected letter easier to complete, review, print and mail. They provide evidence of useful content and consumer demand. They do not by themselves authorize every paid preparation service, confer commercial reproduction rights, determine eligibility, or make mail the right submission channel.

Separate three decisions for each proposed product: lawful provider/service model; rights to reproduce/adapt the specific source; and accurate content, coverage, timing and delivery method. Keep the existing [state launch review](STATE_LAUNCH_REVIEW_2026-10-20.md) open, including California's LDA question, North Carolina's interactive-document requirements, Arizona's preparer framework and national credit-dispute classification. Official wording does not release those review items.

California's [San Mateo court guidance](https://sanmateo.courts.ca.gov/self-help/small-claims-self-help/i-want-sue-small-claims-court-plaintiffs) distinguishes the deposit tool for no refund/itemization after the stated period from disputes about a partial refund, for which it directs users to the general money-demand tool. Treat this as an important branching requirement to verify, rather than assuming one official tool covers every deposit dispute. Its links also identify bad-check and stop-payment demand forms. Those require separate statutory delivery, timing and factual checks; defer them from the first product release.

The California AG's [identity-theft resources](https://oag.ca.gov/idtheft/information-sheets) include a [2013 victim checklist with sample letters](https://oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/privacy/CIS_3_vtm_checklist_10_13.pdf). Age, enclosures, identity documents and the unresolved credit-service model make these unsuitable for automatic launch in the present single-page product.

## California privacy: channel first

The [AG's CCPA guidance](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa) describes access/knowledge, correction, deletion, opt-out and sensitive-information rights, with coverage, verification and exceptions. This review did not find a universal official mailed CCPA form. Business-designated request methods matter. Do not imply that every company must accept a mailed request at its corporate headquarters, that every privacy complaint supports damages, or that dispatch makes DocketStamp an authorized agent. An agent submission would need its own reviewed authorization and verification workflow.

California's [DROP service](https://privacy.ca.gov/drop/) is a free residence-verified mechanism for deletion requests to registered data brokers. It is operating in 2026, with broker processing obligations beginning August 1. A California privacy area should prominently direct matching users to DROP and other free official channels. A targeted request to a particular covered business is a different task. Paid mail should be offered only when the reviewed submission method supports it and its benefit is clear.

Oregon is a particularly useful second privacy candidate because the DOJ supplies an actual letter. Its [current guidance](https://www.doj.state.or.us/consumer-protection/id-theft-data-breaches/privacy/) also describes universal opt-out signals; a paper letter should not displace an appropriate free digital mechanism.

## Reuse and provider classification

The indexed [California Courts terms](https://courts.ca.gov/es/node/33320) restrict downloads to personal, noncommercial use and address permission for copyrighted material beyond fair use. These terms are a specific reason to investigate the source and any third-party tool terms before commercial reproduction. They do not establish that every statutory phrase is copyrighted or that every use is forbidden. Do not assume state-created resources have the same status as federal government works. Record permission/license analysis per source; linking to the free resource is available while reuse remains unresolved.

California BPC §§6400 onward addresses compensated self-help document assistance as discussed in the prior review. The [State Bar statutory compilation](https://www.calbar.ca.gov/sites/default/files/2025-09/Publication-250-Volume-2.pdf) distinguishes published information and ministerial document assistance. It is older material; the current legislative page returned 403. Obtain current operative text and a service-model assessment before calling the paid California product cleared. Original drafting from general legal facts may avoid copying a particular source but does not independently resolve provider classification.

## Proposed California area

Proposed future route: `/california`, with user-selected tasks rather than automated legal recommendations. Suggested first cards are “Demand a refund or repair,” “Demand your security deposit back,” and “Exercise your privacy rights.” Each card identifies the free official resource, DocketStamp's optional service, eligibility facts to check, supported delivery channel, and the source/version date. Do not use court seals, imply endorsement, or describe an adaptation as an official form.

The selected task leads to deterministic questions, applicable state/matter checks, the complete letter preview, consumer adoption, and postal checkout only for an enabled, reviewed mail product. Privacy may instead lead to a free official portal. Price the preparation/printing/postage service transparently; do not imply that the state's free text is exclusive to us.

Before a new card becomes purchasable: review provider and reuse evidence; review current statute and source; define applicability and unsupported branches; verify recipient channel and enclosures; validate one-page geometry and fixed prose; record current digest/review date; explicitly enable the new template. This sprint builds the administration and two geography dimensions, not those new letters or this public route.

## Controls delivered now and next controls

SPRINT-0012 adds per-template and global availability, optional current-review requirement, hold status, source/reuse metadata, content digests, expiry, audit/revision checks, and residence/property all/allow/block rules. Enforcement covers discovery, preparation and new checkout locks. Existing locked orders keep their exact snapshots. Existing templates remain migration defaults until an operator saves policy; an enabled switch is not legal clearance.

Further product work should add reviewed per-template applicability and required delivery channels, source-change monitoring, scheduled effective policies, dual approval for high-impact policy changes, required disclosure/contract acceptance versions, and a separate workflow for intervening in existing orders. Other possible nexus facts (transaction location, provider operations, governing agreement) need individual legal analysis; do not infer them from recipient headquarters or IP. These are a backlog, not implemented controls or newly scheduled automations.

No new geographic restriction, official-source label, approval record, paid template, or live policy write was activated by this research. Coverage is an opportunity scan, not a 50-state conclusion.
