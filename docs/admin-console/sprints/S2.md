## Acceptance gates

- Paid totals remain constant through PROCESSING_DISPATCH, DELIVERED, RETURNED and fulfillment failure.
- Concurrent webhook/confirm duplicate events yield one capture and one obligation; wrong order, amount, currency, mode and replay conflict are rejected/quarantined.
- Refund/dispute adjustments trace to unique cash IDs; fee unavailable stays null; no actual money movement is initiated.
- Webhook acknowledgment follows durable receipt; outbox failure is visible and safely retriable; analytics failure cannot erase payment.
