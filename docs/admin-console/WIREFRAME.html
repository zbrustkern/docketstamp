Investigation and control design

Expand sections to inspect the proposed evidence and command behavior. These are design annotations, not working application controls.

Scope, authority and freshness

Environment, payment mode, Lob mode and CASS mode are distinct. Every panel reports source, definition version, time basis, as-of, watermark, coverage and exclusions. Date intervals are [start, end). Operational obligations remain all-open and visibly labeled.

Loading → skeleton; no data → no qualifying records; unavailable → not measured; error → last successful evidence with retry; stale → age badge; partial → coverage warning and suppressed unsupported comparisons.

Order investigation and safe retry

One timeline: frozen template/version/hash → capture/refund/dispute → authorized dispatch attempts → accepted letter → mailed / scans / return receipt → receipt-email results. Private document/address detail requires authorization and logged reveal.

Command preview: actor, order version, exact snapshot hash, paid/free authority, Lob mode, cost uncertainty, provider reconciliation, idempotency key and reason. “Reconcile” records evidence only. Resubmit is unavailable until ambiguity is resolved. Address correction cannot silently alter signed content.

Supporting journey and campaign evidence

Paginated events with observed/server/provider authority, received/source time, classification, missing prerequisites and dedupe result. Converting sessions and order counts remain separate. Original acquisition, latest acquisition and offer touch history coexist. No document values, tokens, search strings or raw URLs.

Money, costs and reconciliation

Verified captures, successful refunds, dispute cash adjustments, actual fees and supplier charges/credits. Evidence rows preserve currency and provider IDs privately. A returned notice remains a paid order. Missing costs/spend are unavailable; estimated tier costs belong in a labeled scenario. Every reconciliation run has scope, cursor, unmatched facts and completion evidence.

Offers and recovery

Keep percentage/fixed/100% codes and comps. Composer shows all tier prices, eligibility, quota/budget/reservations/expiry and economic coverage. Canonical offer links accept legacy discount/promo aliases, persist through navigation/resume, validate server-side and show the applied price. Zero-total fulfillment remains separate from paid conversion.

Recovery links use an actual validated eligible offer. No automatic outreach. Exposure → apply → reserve → pay/free → refund → fulfillment is reporting, not proof of incremental lift.

Application controls and decision log

Role-checked allowlisted controls show actual consumers and effective environment. Record actor, diff, reason, version, audit and rollback. No green health based on NODE_ENV or incident absence. Decisions record hypothesis, evidence, owner, evaluation date and guardrails; simulations never masquerade as reported financials.

DocketStamp is an automated self-help document preparation and USPS postal dispatch utility, not a law firm. No legal outcome is inferred by these proposed metrics. See full specification and contracts.
