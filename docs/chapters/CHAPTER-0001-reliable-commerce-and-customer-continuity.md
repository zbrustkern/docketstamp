## Design Direction

### Customer continuity

Keep one chooser and one preparation journey with direct company, campaign and agent entry. Provide one understandable navigation model across forms, review and checkout. Browser traversal, refresh, retry and exploration preserve compatible work. Browsing candidates and examples does not commit destructive draft changes. Agent hydration and delayed requests cannot overwrite newer user edits.

Retain optional accounts and guest checkout. Tab-scoped recovery restores inputs, not signature or verification authority. Cross-session saving is an explicit capability with retention and access control. Address suggestions explain the correction and never imply legal eligibility or a contractually correct destination.

Make final review physically faithful. Share geometry and typography as well as text, preserve fixed print layout on mobile, and bind review to the frozen document's actual identity and date. Keep customer guidance plain and all required disclosures intact.

### Operating evidence

Lead the console with customer payments, open mailing obligations, observed progression, contribution and evidence freshness. Keep estimates useful and visibly modeled; preserve missing/incomplete states where needed. Define cohorts, classification, reporting timezone, cost coverage and attribution before interpreting conversion or acquisition return.

Connect exceptions to a next action and owner. Payment received, supplier submission, postal events and receipt artifacts are separate facts. Recovery reconciles uncertainty before another irreversible action. Retain enough evidence to explain each customer's payment and mailing without exposing document details to analytics.

## Architecture Principles

1. **The server owns irreversible authority.** Bind checkout attempt, quote, exact snapshot, signature, address evidence and payment. Derive ownership from verified identity or a scoped guest capability. Cancellation races record actual provider outcomes and cannot silently substitute edited material.
2. **Frozen content remains immutable.** Supersession changes attempt lifecycle metadata; old document bytes and financial facts remain intact. Existing locks retain the specified policy semantics. New work evaluates current policy transactionally.
3. **One authorized fulfillment path.** Converge paid, free, comp and operator recovery around existing durable obligations and dispatch safeguards. Preserve stable provider idempotency, leases, uncertain states and explicit reconciliation; migrate current entry points deliberately.
4. **Shared boundaries precede shared packages.** Reuse tested contracts, failure cases and runbooks across DocketStamp and SendMyNotes. Keep Lob and Handwrytten behavior, credentials, data access and product rules separate. Extract common code after both implementations establish a stable contract.
5. **Privacy covers the entire journey.** Minimize telemetry values, third-party purchase metadata, URLs, referrers, logs and browser storage. Opaque stage/access links are capabilities. Human signature and payment remain required for agent-prepared notices.
6. **Every release is identifiable and reversible.** Prefer deployable trunk, small branches, server runtime gates, backward-compatible migrations and exact served-build evidence. Use scoped cherry-picking to escape existing divergence without making it the permanent release model.

## Review Posture

Evaluate progress through observable outcomes: work survives Back and retry; a payment authorizes one exact snapshot; no timeout causes a blind duplicate mailing; the paper matches review; operators can explain money and obligations; acquisition decisions account for cost and evidence coverage.

Require tests where races or authority matter, browser evidence where interaction matters, and provider test proofs where physical output matters. Keep simulated, provider-test and live results distinguishable. Do not infer commercial success from clicks, local passing tests or a staged feature.

## Non-Goals

This chapter does not authorize new prices, international mail, multi-page notices, new statutory templates or prose, legal outcome claims, automatic agent signing/payment, or a shared database between the businesses. It changes no mandatory disclosure and schedules no deployment or recurring job.
